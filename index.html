    Help & Guide

    Contents

    The Home Page

    1. Start a ride: share a taxi from the landing up to take-off.
    2. Join: hop into a ride that's going.
    3. The weather now. Tap it for the full Weather page.
    4. Bottom bar: Plan, the green home button (it also starts a ride), Menu.

    The painting is the Bir landing, live. Tap ⤢ or turn your phone sideways to see it full screen.

    Plan

    1. Each coming day: the forecast and who's going up. Tap a day to add your plan.

    Menu

    1. Weather, Safety & Contacts, Plan your travel, Getting Around Bir.

    ⚙ at the top right of every page: your name, settings, Help & Guide, Feedback.

    Your Name and WhatsApp Number

    1. ⚙ → tap your name at the top.
    2. Change your name, add your number, or turn "Share my number" off.

    Your name shows on your rides. Your number is private: only people in the same ride see it, and it's wiped after the ride. Keep it out of the name box.

    Put Ride Up on Your Home Screen

    1. ⚙ → Add to Home Screen.
    2. iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Add to Home Screen.

    It then opens full screen, like an app. No app store.

    Try It First with Bot Rides

    1. Home page → Try it with bot rides 🤖. Join one, or start your own and watch the bots join.

    Pretend rides, only on your phone. They go away when you reload or tap Remove bots.

    Start a Ride

    Tap Start a ride on the home page.

    1. The car: Alto, 4 seats. Tap change? for another.
    2. The seats. Yours is the first.
    3. Friends with you? Tap +1, +2 or +3.
    4. Tap Start ride. Others see it at once.
    5. Driving your own car? Offer a lift instead.

    It leaves from the LZ taxi stand. Leaving later or from somewhere else? Add a note.

    Change the Car

    1. Pick Alto, Eeco, Bolero, or Other: type the car, set the seats, tap ✓.

    Starting a ride: tap change?. Already started: ⋯ on your ride → Change car. Only the person who started it can.

    Leaving Time or Pick-up: Add a Note

    ⋯ on your ride → Add a note.

    1. Tap a quick pick ("At the taxi stand", "Leaving in 5 min"…).
    2. Or type your own: "Leaving at 10:30", "Pick me up at Charlie's". Tap Save.

    Everyone in the ride sees it. Joining and want a pick-up on the way? Join, then add your own note.

    Your Ride: Share It to the Group

    1. WhatsApp the people in your ride.
    2. Share to group: WhatsApp opens with a link. Pick the group, send.
    3. ⋯: more (see the next point).
    4. We've driven off: tap it when the taxi leaves.

    Whoever taps the link lands on your ride and can join.

    Join a Ride

    Tap Join on a ride.

    1. The seats. Yours fills in.
    2. Friends with you? Tap +1, +2 or +3.
    3. Tap Join. Your seat fills on everyone's phone.

    Reach the others with the WhatsApp or call buttons on the ride.

    Inside Your Ride: the ⋯ Menu

    1. Add a friend.
    2. Add a note: leaving time, pick-up.
    3. Change car.
    4. We've driven off.
    5. Cancel my seat. The last one out ends the ride.

    Also there: Share my number with this ride, and Happy to leave with fewer (you'll go even if seats stay empty).

    When the Ride Is Full

    1. Your full ride stays on screen until someone taps We've driven off (2 hours at most).

    Everyone else sees it as Full for 30 min.

    We've Driven Off, or I Didn't Go

    1. When the taxi leaves, one of you taps We've driven off. The ride moves to "Left in the last 30 min".
    2. Tapped it by mistake, or left behind? Start or join another ride, then tap I didn't go.

    Offer a Lift in Your Own Car

    1. Start a ride → Offer a lift (at the bottom).
    2. Room for 1, 2 or 3. Tap Offer the lift.
    3. Change the pick-up place or time later from ⋯.

    For sharing a ride, not for paid rides.

    Plan Your Flying Day

    1. Tap Plan, then a day: the forecast, and who's going up when.
    2. + Add my plan → pick a time → Add my plan.
    3. Share the day to the WhatsApp group when it asks.

    Going Later Today? Use Plan

    A ride is for now. For "2 spots at 3 pm":

    1. Plan → today → pick the time → Add my plan.
    2. Close to the time, Start a ride and share it.

    Weather at Take-off and the Landing

    1. Tap TAKE-OFF or LANDING to switch; swipe the hours.

    Open it from the home page weather, or Menu → Weather. It's a forecast, not a briefing: watch the sky, ask local pilots.

    Sightline: See the Landing from Take-off?

    1. Sightline to the landing: the forecast cloud between take-off and the landing. Tap an hour to see it then.
    2. Report: tell others what you see.

    Ticks and crosses are pilots' reports. Tap the title for what it means, and a Windy link.

    Report What You See

    1. Pick where you are: TAKE-OFF or LANDING.
    2. Tap clear if you can see across, foggy if you can't.

    Your name shows; your number stays private.

    Safety and Contacts

    1. Menu → Safety & Contacts.
    2. Tap a number to call: 112, rescue, hospitals, helplines. Radio: 144.500 MHz.

    Ride Up isn't a rescue service: call first.

    Find Travel Plans

    Menu → Plan your travel.

    1. The calendar: a green dot = seats on offer, an orange dot = people asking. Tap a day.
    2. Everyone else's plans for that day.

    Join Someone's Travel Plan

    1. Tap a plan to see who's going, the seats and the stops.
    2. Tap Join this plan, then Join & share numbers.

    You both see each other's numbers: sort out the rest on WhatsApp. Changed your mind? Open it → Leave this trip.

    Add Your Travel Plan

    1. Tap Add your travel plan.
    2. From, To, When, How: share a taxi, a booked taxi with seats, or your own car.
    3. More options: how many, pick-ups on the way (type one, tap Save), your car.
    4. Tap Save.

    Map of Bir

    1. Menu → Getting Around Bir.
    2. Pick a list: Key places, Medical, ATMs…
    3. Zoom with − / + or the slider. The layers button (top right) switches map and satellite.
    4. Tap a pin to open it in Google Maps.

    Your Settings

    1. ⚙ → your name: name, colour, number, your car.
    2. Display & units: light or dark, km/h or knots, °C or °F, 12 h or 24 h.
    3. Newest version: pull down from the top of a page and let go.

    Common Questions

    Account? None. Your name is kept on this phone.

    Free? Yes, no ads. By flyers, for flyers.

    A ride vanished? A waiting ride closes 30 min after the last join. Rides that left show at the bottom for 30 min.

    Still need the WhatsApp group? Yes: chat there, keep the seats here.

    "No signal" or "Weather unavailable"? Weak signal or a busy service. Try again in a moment.

    Knots or °F? ⚙ → your name → Display & units.

    Something broken? ⚙ → Feedback. We read every message.

    Origin Story

    Paragliding at Bir is a quiet kind of magic: a wing, the wind and your spirit, gliding high above the sweeping valleys of the Dhauladhar range.

    Bir-Billing, the paragliding capital of India, is often called the world's second-highest paragliding site, and Asia's highest. Take-off at Billing sits a thousand metres above a landing ground of cafés, cows and windsocks, where paragliding enthusiasts from around the world end up sharing chai and stories by evening. It starts feeling like home surprisingly fast.

    Flying here is nothing short of divine; the long, winding taxi ride up to take-off at Billing… eh, not so much. Sharing it with fellow pilots simply makes sense. Finding them, though, means a WhatsApp group: someone has to add you first, and then your "anyone going up?" sinks under a hundred other messages.

    With the sugar of flying, the spice of finding a shared ride up and everything nice about Bir, a like-minded paragliding enthusiast imagined a solution and took up vibe-coding to build it. And so a web-based, no-sign-up, no-ads, zero-nonsense ride-sharing Community Tool was born!

    Ride Up: made by flyers, for flyers. Just open one page, see who's waiting, and join in to share a taxi.

    If it makes life a little better, use it, share it, help improve it, and make paragliding at Bir even more magical!

    P.S. If you code for real, design, or simply have ideas, reach out and join the project.

    Community Code

    Same sky, same field. A few simple things:

    😊Be kind. Everyone's here for the same sky.
    🚕Only start a ride you're really in, and tap "We've driven off" when you go.
    🚫No touting or advertising taxis, tandems or anything else.
    🔒Never share someone else's number.
    🐄Respect the landing ground, the locals and the animals on it. And wave at the cows.

    No accounts. No ads. No tracking of you. Your number disappears when your ride's done, and everything else within a week. We only keep anonymous counts of how the app is used.

    Terms of Use

    • Ride Up helps people find others to share a ride. It isn't a taxi operator: it doesn't book, run or pay for rides, and it isn't responsible for any ride, driver, vehicle or arrangement.

    • You choose who you ride with. Use your own judgement.

    • A lift in a private car is for sharing a ride, not for running paid rides in a private vehicle.

    • The weather and the mood line are a feel from a forecast model — not a flying briefing. Always check conditions on site, with local pilots and the Billing Paragliding Association.

    • Ride Up isn't a rescue service. In an emergency, call 112.

    • Fake, offensive or advertising posts may be removed. Ride Up is free, offered as it is, and may change or be unavailable at times.

    Privacy Notes

    • No sign-up. Your name and number are saved on this device so you don't have to retype them.

    • Your WhatsApp number is shared only with people in the same ride or trip, and only if you've added it. "Share my number" is on to start with; turn it off any time in Settings. A lift always shares it with whoever joins.

    • Numbers are wiped automatically within an hour of the ride being over.

    • Sky reports show your name. Your number goes with a report only so one number can't send more than 3 an hour; nobody sees it, and it's deleted within 2 hours.

    • Everything else — rides, trips, names, notes, plans and sky reports — is deleted automatically a week after it's over. While it's live, names and notes are visible to anyone using Ride Up.

    • Feedback is kept so the team can read it, and only the team can see it. A name or number you add to it is removed after 90 days.

    • No cookies. The only thing kept on your device is your own name, number, car, friends' names, notes and settings, and the rides you're part of — "Delete saved data" (Settings → your name) clears it.

    • Anonymous usage counts. To see what's useful, the app notes things like "the map was opened" or "a ride was started", with the app's version, and a short note if something breaks. Never your name, number, location or anything that points to you or your device. These counts are deleted after six months.

    • Want something deleted sooner? Send a note through Settings → Feedback, or email us (Settings → Reach Out).
