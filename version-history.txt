RIDE UP — VERSION HISTORY
Share a taxi from the Bir landing ground up to take-off.
Made by flyers, for flyers at Bir-Billing, Himachal Pradesh, India.

How versions are numbered
- 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 ... are releases that people see.
- 1.0.1, 1.0.2 ... are small fixes.
- 2.0 would be a big change in how people use Ride Up.

Newest first. Dates and times are Bir time (India).


RIDE UP 1.0.1 — 00:46 · 03.11.2026
The first round of pilots' feedback, answered. Thanks to everyone on the group who tried it on day one.
- Full rides: a full ride no longer disappears after 10 minutes. It stays with the people in it until someone taps
  "We've driven off" (2 hours at most); everyone else sees it as Full for 30 minutes.
- Change car: ⋯ → Change car now offers Alto / Eeco / Other + seats again (it opened the wrong pop-up).
- Notes for leaving time and pick-up points are explained everywhere; rides leave from the LZ taxi stand.
- Names: a phone number typed into the name box is moved to the private number box, so it's never shown.
- Help & Guide (⚙ → Help & Guide): every workflow step by step with screenshots, one page with contents, search,
  and links that open a topic (rideup.pages.dev/#help-notes). The FAQ moved here from About. "See how …" links on
  the Weather, Plan, Plan your travel pages and the Start a ride pop-up.
- Plan your travel: no longer breaks when "Share my car details" is tapped; Save buttons on the typed pick-up and
  count boxes; the selected day is centred; a save with no signal stops after 20 seconds with a clear message.
- Pull to refresh: pull down from the top of a page (or in the full-screen painting) to get the newest version.
- Sideways: turning the phone on the home page opens the full-screen painting; Help & Guide works sideways too;
  the rest of the app asks you to turn the phone upright.
- iPhone Home Screen app: a solid status bar, so the top of the pages is no longer blurred (iOS 26+).
- Map: zoom stops where satellite pictures exist; solid zoom buttons and a bigger slider handle.
- Scenery: the night-time phone glow is a tiny light again (it had grown into a pale disc); the scenery no longer
  piles up and restarts all at once after the app was in the background.
- Settings: Help & Guide sits just above Feedback. About carries a one-line note of what changed.


RIDE UP 1.0 — 17:00 · 01.11.2026
The first release for pilots at Bir.
- Now: start a taxi ride from the landing or hop into one; seats fill live on everyone's phone. Bring friends,
  add a note, change the car, leave with fewer, "We've driven off", and "I didn't go".
- Offer a lift: someone's driving you up and there's room? Offer the seats from the Landing area, leaving now.
- Plan: say when you're heading up to fly in the next days, see who else is, and share your plan to WhatsApp.
- Plan your travel: the whole month on a calendar of trips to and from Bir; ask for a ride or offer seats (taxi
  or your own car, with your car details if you like), and join others; numbers are swapped once you're in.
- Weather: Billing take-off and the Bir landing in one card with the next hours (swipe them, tap the place to
  switch). "Sightline to the landing": a painted side view showing the forecast's cloud against the line of
  sight, with ticks and crosses from people up there. Report what you see in two taps (with your saved name
  and number). The forecast note links to Windy.
- Feedback: a short chat with choices to tap, or just type.
- Sharing: rides and plans go to WhatsApp with a short link; the preview shows the app icon and says what it is
  (e.g. "Alto · 2 seats left · ~9:10 am" or "Mon 2 Nov · 3 pilots heading up").
- Getting around Bir: a map of key places, medical, ATMs, groceries and hostels.
- Safety & contacts: emergency, rescue and hospital numbers, and the 144.500 MHz safety frequency.
- The scenery: a painted view of the landing ground through the day, true to the real terrain, with gliders,
  tandems landing into the live wind, cows, cafés, the road and its traffic.
- The app icon: the painted Bir sky with two gliders, and a white Alto taxi with wing bags on the roof, close up.
- Bot rides: try everything with pretend rides that exist only on your phone.
- Privacy: no accounts, no ads, no tracking; numbers shared only inside a ride and wiped when it's over.
